3. Intellectual Property and Frameworks
3.1 G.A.P.S. Framework (Current / Active Development)
The G.A.P.S. (Gratitude, Acceptance, Presence, Surrender) Framework is the primary stabilization model under active development by HALO Antifragile AI.
•	Designed as a non-clinical, client-facing stabilization and conscious-choice framework
•	Intended to support individuals experiencing high stress, crisis transition, or post-crisis instability
•	Not a treatment, diagnosis, or replacement for healthcare services
•	Operates as a complementary psychoeducational and self-regulation support
The G.A.P.S. framework is supported by original written materials and an academic clinical paper documenting its theoretical grounding and development methodology.
